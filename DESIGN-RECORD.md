# DESIGN RECORD — TEMPLATE 2: TRIVIA NIGHT HOSTING COMPANY
**Brand Name:** Brainwave Trivia — Live Social Trivia & Pub Quiz Experiences  
**Industry:** Entertainment / Social Pub Trivia / Corporate & Private Quiz Events  

---

## 1. Business Requirements & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:**
  1. *Players & Teams:* Friends, pub regulars, corporate coworkers, pop culture fans looking for fun weekly live social experiences.
  2. *Pub & Venue Owners:* Bar, taproom, and restaurant managers wanting to pack slow weeknights (Tuesdays/Wednesdays) with high-spend trivia crowds.
  3. *Corporate Event Planners:* HR and team leads booking interactive team-building and custom corporate quiz celebrations.
- **Tone & Mood:** High-energy, social, electric, playful, community-driven, modern, crisp, professional.
- **Key Rules:**
  - NO DASHBOARD.
  - NO Login/Register pages.
  - Exactly 7 Main Navbar items (Home with Home 1 & Home 2 dropdown, Schedule, Venues & Cities, Corporate & Private, Host a Trivia Night, About, Contact).
  - Absolutely UNIQUE imagery across all pages (zero image reuse).

---

## 2. Design System & Visual Identity (100% Distinct from Template 1)

| Design Aspect | Template 1 (Murder Mystery Dinner Theater) | Template 2 (Brainwave Trivia Night Co.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aesthetic Personality** | Luxury Noir, Gothic Mystery, Candlelit Grandeur | Vibrant Modern Social, Electric Cyber-Pub, High-Energy Bento |
| **Primary Colors** | Obsidian Noir (`#08090C`), Pure Gold (`#FFB800`), Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`) | Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`), Neon Sky (`#0284C7`), Deep Slate Navy (`#070D18`) |
| **Accent Colors** | Amber Crimson Glow (`#FFE082`) | Cyber Mint (`#10B981`), Vivid Coral (`#FF4D4D`), Deep Indigo (`#4F46E5`) |
| **Color Separation** | Gold/Amber Luxury Noir | **ZERO Gold/Yellow** — Pure Electric Cyan + Cyber Mint + Coral |
| **Typography Display** | `Cinzel Decorative`, `Playfair Display` (Serif) | `Outfit` (Ultra-modern geometric Sans-Serif, 700-900) |
| **Typography Body** | `Plus Jakarta Sans` | `Inter` (Clean high-legibility Sans-Serif) |
| **Card System** | Gold-bordered noir cards with dark gradients | Crisp bento cards, cyan/mint glowing borders, live pulse dots |
| **Button Style** | Sharp rectangular gold borders with subtle bevel | Curved high-glow cyan & mint pill buttons, tactile micro-lift |
| **Navbar Style** | Gothic center luxury nav with reservations CTA | Clean centered glassmorphic bar, 7 structured pages, Theme & RTL |
| **Footer Style** | Dramatic theatrical columns with ornate dividers | 4-column balanced tech/lifestyle social footer |
