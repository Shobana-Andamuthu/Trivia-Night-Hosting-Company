Our Core Philosophy

The 4 Pillars of Brainwave Entertainment

We believe pub trivia is not an academic exam—it is a communal celebration of laughter, friendly rivalry, and great craft beer.

PILLAR 01

Laughter Over Memorization

Every round is written with comedic twists, clever wordplay, and multimedia sound clips. If your team does not know the answer, you will still laugh at the clue options.

PILLAR 02

Zero Paper, Zero Waiting

Our cloud smartphone buzzers provide instant automated grading and live big-screen leaderboards. No walking crumpled paper slips up to the bar or 20-minute scoring delays.

PILLAR 03

The Anti-Googling Guarantee

We design visual anagrams, rapid-fire speed audio clips, and 25-second response timers that make smartphone cheating mathematically impossible and entirely pointless.

PILLAR 04

Supporting Local Taprooms

We partner exclusively with independent craft breweries, neighborhood taverns, and local community spaces, packing their slowest weeknights with loyal recurring players.

Our Journey

From One Dive Bar to a National League

How a grassroots Tuesday night experiment evolved into a premier national live trivia organization.

2018
THE ORIGIN

The First Tuesday Experiment

Two Austin standup comedians host the very first Brainwave game at an East Austin taproom. 18 players show up—by week 4, the bar is at 100% capacity.

2020
DIGITAL BUZZER LAUNCH

The Cloud Buzzer Revolution

We retire paper score sheets forever and build our proprietary zero-app smartphone buzzer engine, allowing instant scoring and nationwide live Zoom tournaments.

2022
NATIONAL EXPANSION

Denver, Seattle & Chicago Expansion

Brainwave expands into Denver, Seattle, Nashville, and Chicago craft brewery networks, recruiting top local standup comedic talent as regional emcees.

2024
CORPORATE DIVISION

Corporate All-Hands & Charity Series

Launched our dedicated corporate team battle division, hosting custom all-hands game shows for Google, Stripe, and over 450 tech companies.

2026
THE NATIONAL STAGE

50+ Taprooms & Annual Championship Series

Over 12,000 players battle weekly across 50+ partner taprooms, competing for the annual $10,000 Brainwave National Trophy Tournament.

Fair Play & Community

The Brainwave Player Code of Conduct

Four simple rules that ensure every player, host, and bartender enjoys a safe, fair, and exhilarating night.

01

The Anti-Googling Pact

Phones are for submitting buzzer answers only. Shouting answers or searching Google ruins the magic for everyone.

02

Tip Your Bartenders & Servers

Our venue partners provide free table space. Support their staff generously with food and drink orders.

03

Friendly Rivalry Only

Cheer on your neighboring tables, celebrate close buzzer battles, and keep banter playful and inclusive.

04

The Emcee's Ruling Is Final

Our comedy hosts make calls in good faith. If you have a legitimate scoring question, approach politely during intermission.
